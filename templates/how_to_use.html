Documentation & Guidance

Master the Statistics Toolkit and our AI-augmented features.

The AI Wizard

Our flagship feature. If you're unsure which statistical test to use, simply describe your data and goals in the Wizard. It will recommend the most appropriate method based on your group count and data types.

Calculation Modules

Descriptive Statistics

Input a list of numbers to get Mean, Median, Std Dev, and automatically identify outliers using the IQR method.

Linear Regression

Input X and Y pairs to find the line of best fit. The tool provides the equation, R², and an interactive scatter plot.

ANOVA

Format your data as group:1,2,3;group:4,5,6 to compare means across multiple populations with Effect Size (η², ω²) analysis.

Normal Distribution

Input observed values, mean, and standard deviation to calculate Z-scores and P-values instantly.

Pro Tips

  • Use Sample Datasets from the datasets page to test logic before entering large datasets.
  • All AI insights are generated locally via Ollama—your data never leaves your computer.
  • Hover over Chart Points in regression and descriptive plots for exact values.
  • Check your History to compare results from different parameters side-by-side.
